TECHNICAL DESIGN AND DIFFERENTIATION

Explainable recovery, with boundaries.

The Google stack

  • Cloud Run: FastAPI application and web interface.
  • Gemini through Vertex AI or the Gemini API: schema-constrained next-step proposals.
  • Runtime service identity: Google credentials stay on the server.
  • Independent controller: permissions, versions, idempotency and final checks.

What we can defend

  • A working observation–replanning loop with execution receipts.
  • Explicit escalation when evidence is unknown or dispatch is irreversible.
  • Six reviewed scenarios and automated controller/browser checks.
  • Inspectable comparison with a guarded fixed workflow.

Retries and compensation are established techniques. A broader rules engine can also solve these fixtures; incremental AI benefit requires live evaluation and wider semantic variation.
